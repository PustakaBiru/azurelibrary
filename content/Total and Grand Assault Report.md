# Notes on Notation
## Star
Star level of student that has reached 5\* mystic are written by the maximum level of their Exclusive Weapon, as follows:

| Exclusive Weapon Stars | Maximum Weapon Level | Star Notation |
| :--------------------: | :------------------: | :-----------: |
|          1\*           |          30          |     UE30      |
|          2\*           |          40          |     UE40      |
|          3\*           |          50          |     UE50      |
|          4\*           |          60          |     UE60      |
## Skills and Gears
Skill levels and gear tiers are written as ABCD, as follows:

| Notation |  Skill   |   Gear   |
| :------: | :------: | :------: |
|    A     |    EX    | 1st slot |
|    B     |  Basic   | 2nd slot |
|    C     | Enhanced | 3rd slot |
|    D     |   Sub    |  Unique  |

>[!info] Notes on non-number notation
>- Skill level 10 (maximum level) is written as M.
>- Gear tier 10 is written as X.
>- Lack of (or unequipped) unique gear is written as hyphen (-).
## Limit Break
Limit break are written as A/B/C, denoting limit break level of Maximum HP, ATK, and Healing, respectively. Student under 5\* has their limit break written as -/-/-.
## Bond
If a student has multiple version of outfits, her Relationship Rank is displayed as a/b/c (number of letters depending on the number of alts). Each letter represents the Relationship Rank of the student in a specific outfit, ordered by release date. Bold number indicates the Relationship Rank of the student deployed on the team. If I don't have a specific student alt, her Relationship Rank is written as a hyphen (-).
>[!example]
>I am using Hina (Dress) in Total Assault. She has two alts, (base) Hina and Hina (Swimsuit), the latter of which I don't have. The Relationship Rank of Hina and Hina (Dress) is 21 and 46, respectively. Then, the Relationship Rank for Hina (Dress) is 21/-/**46**.
