# Introduction
The Total Assault on Yesod, Blue Archive's newest boss, has ended on March 24th. Ever since this boss was released, I've been hyping up Yesod. Why? Because, back when it was released in Japan server, I have all the students required to clear this boss at Lunatic.[^1]

Just like Geburah, this raid kicked a lot of people's asses. Unlike Geburah, it's not because of the student requirements, but because people went into the raid without reading the boss skill description or raid guide. Either way, the end result is the same — in Asia server (where I play), the Platinum cutoff is at middle Extreme clear.[^2]

![[20260328235458_1.jpg]]

Up until Torment, everything goes fine for me with this boss. However, he starts to kick my ass at Lunatic difficulty, because I can't for the love of God consistently execute the Wakamo team for Phase 1. Despite that, I still managed to beat Yesod Lunatic, even if only once and with five minutes left before the ticket timer runs out.

While the "standard" three-team clear used this team as the second one, I use this team first. This is for two reason:
- Swimsuit Mika's EX doesn't depend on the boss' remaining HP, so swapping the order won't change the amount of damage she deals.
- Since all strikers has Special or Elastic armor, this team is easier to do than the second one, freeing up time to mald on the second one.

When running this team, I need to carefully check Kokona's Basic Skill usage before using her EX to prevent canceling her Stamp of Praise stack. Since she only heals herself on EX and when her Sub Skill triggers, canceling her stack means she won't reach 5 stacks at the intended time, delaying her self-heal and risking her retreat from Yesod's attack. Doing this takes some practice, but it’s really not very difficult to do — just be careful.

This team can do 24 million damage, give or take 1 million.

This team is absolutely miserable to play.

First, I have to do an unbelievably many EX skills in a four and half minute period. If I count correctly, I have to do 92 EX skills in this team — more than twice the average EX skills usage in Torment raid, meaning twice the opportunity to make mistake.

Second, keeping Wakamo alive throughout the entire raid is viciously hard. Some of the Idol Mari's EX skills are targeted at her and require *milliseconds* timing, or she would retreat from Yesod's attack. Even that is still isn't enough — in some of my runs, the difference in Idol Mari's EX timing (still in milliseconds, mind you) will result in Kisaki's Basic Skill going to Swimsuit Hanako instead of Wakamo. If that happens, the last Yesod attack just before the third wave will make Wakamo retreat.

You may notice that I decided to raise Swimsuit Hanako to UE60, instead of Swimsuit Mika or Wakamo, which are the main dealers. There's a reason for this: unbuffed UE50 Swimsuit Hanako occasionally *fails* to kill the first lantern in one EX. And by fails, I mean like a third to half of the time. Even then, at UE60, she can still somehow fail to do that, although it rarely happens at this level of investment.

This team can do 36 million damage, give or take 1 million.

# Afterword
This is the most expensive raid I've ever done. My main source of expenses is raising Dealers' mystic to UE50 and raising Haruka (New Year), Kaede, and Mari from scratch. In the end, I spent around 200 million credits and 3,500 eligmas, leaving me at the end of the raid with only 20 million or so credits and around 1,300 eligmas.

The next raid is Hieronymus Grand Assault. Based on my research, I can do double torment[^3] without spending any credits at all. I hope I don't spend too many resources on the next raid.
